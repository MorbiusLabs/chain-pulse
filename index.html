Is your bank down?
Check if the chains are.

Live block production for Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana and Base, straight from public nodes. Refreshes every few seconds. Nothing to install, nothing sent anywhere but the nodes themselves.

Checking chains…

How status works. LIVE means the newest block is younger than three times the chain's normal block time (Bitcoin: younger than 60 minutes, since its gaps vary a lot). SLOW means older than that. STALLED means older than ten times the normal time. Block times come from the block itself.

Sources. Bitcoin: mempool.space API. Ethereum, Base: public JSON-RPC at publicnode.com. Solana: getRecentPerformanceSamples on solana-rpc.publicnode.com. If a node is unreachable the card says so; that is a problem with the node, not proof the chain is down.
