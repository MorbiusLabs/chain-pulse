How status works. LIVE: the newest block is younger than 3x the chain's normal block time, with a floor of 15 seconds for fast chains (Bitcoin: younger than 60 minutes). SLOW: older than that. STALLED: older than 10x, at least 60 seconds (Bitcoin: 2 hours). Solana is LIVE above 60 slots per minute.
Sources. Bitcoin: mempool.space, fallback blockstream.info. Other chains: public JSON-RPC, mostly publicnode.com, with official or 1rpc.io fallbacks. Each card shows which node answered. Prices: CoinGecko. A failing node is not proof a chain is down.
Fees. Gas price comes from eth_gasPrice. "≈" is a plain 21,000-gas transfer on Layer 1 EVM chains, a 140 vB payment at the next-block rate on Bitcoin, and the 5,000-lamport base fee on Solana. Layer 2 fees add an L1 data fee, so they show gas price only.
